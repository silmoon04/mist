# Game face animation research

Research collected on 29 September 2026.

This board gathers real game and animator references for the next MIST acting studies. Credits follow creator portfolios, official game material and direct interviews. Each observation separates a sampled visual reading from a creator's account of the work. MIST adaptations are our proposals.

The supplied GIFs and clips let you judge the motion directly. Still inspection does not establish exact blink timing, frame counts or the feel of a full performance.

## Later Alligator / Sweet Geraldine

Large directional eyes and a small curved mouth make a boastful dialogue pose legible without a large facial rig.

- [Lindsay Small-Butera](https://community.wacom.com/en-gb/later-alligator-a-qa-with-the-small-buteras/): Director; writing, concepting, storyboarding, animation and design (self-described in interview)
- [Alex Small-Butera](https://community.wacom.com/en-gb/later-alligator-a-qa-with-the-small-buteras/): Keyframer; animation and design (described by Lindsay in joint interview)

- In the inspected Sweet Geraldine frame, huge eyes look upward while the small mouth curves up toward the snout. A fang and tilted face add personality; the pose reads alongside her boast about being a claw-machine mastermind. [visual observation](https://community.wacom.com/en-gb/later-alligator-a-qa-with-the-small-buteras/)
- Lindsay says the reaction sets were decided case by case around each alligator's conversation nodes, with shared categories including wins, losses, happy, sad, disappointed and angry. [creator statement](https://community.wacom.com/en-gb/later-alligator-a-qa-with-the-small-buteras/)
- The duo describe getting the feeling of the gesture down rapidly and refining it in later passes. Alex describes exporting labeled PNG sequences from Harmony for assembly in Unity. [creator statement](https://community.wacom.com/en-gb/later-alligator-a-qa-with-the-small-buteras/)

MIST experiments:

- Our inference: give MIST distinct confident, sheepish and uncertain resting mouth shapes before adding movement. The eyes should establish attention, with the mouth providing attitude.
- Our inference: author reactions to concrete events such as recognition, misunderstanding and successful completion. Reuse broad emotion categories but choose a different held pose for each event.

Sources:

- [Later Alligator: A Q&A with the Small-Buteras](https://community.wacom.com/en-gb/later-alligator-a-qa-with-the-small-buteras/)
- [Chomping through Later Alligator with Lindsay Small-Butera](https://www.toonboom.com/later-alligator-lindsay-small-butera)

Direct GIF returned HTTP 200 image/gif and was visually inspected through a browser screenshot. Only a sampled still was inspected; no claims about timing, blink cadence or transitions. Joint authorship is established, but the individual animator of this exact GIF is not identified.

## Slime Rancher / six faces

A developer-supplied six-face GIF shows how much expression can fit on the same nearly featureless round body.

- [Ian McConville](https://store.steampowered.com/news/posts/?appids=433340&enddate=1576525914&feed=steam_community_announcements): Principal Artist; author of the Slime Rancher 1.4.0 face-shader rebuild (the post spells the title 'Principle Artist')

- In the inspected GIF frame, the happy bottom-centre face uses dot eyes and a broad open mouth; the worried bottom-right face retains tiny eyes but replaces that mouth with a small downturned shape. The body design stays consistent. [visual observation](https://store.steampowered.com/news/posts/?appids=433340&enddate=1576525914&feed=steam_community_announcements)
- The startled bottom-left face enlarges the eye marks and uses a small open mouth. This is an expression-shape comparison, not a verified timing analysis. [visual observation](https://store.steampowered.com/news/posts/?appids=433340&enddate=1576525914&feed=steam_community_announcements)
- McConville describes separate eye and mouth channels in an RGBA distance-field mask, mouth-size changes through smoothstep, and UV noise for face wobble. He says swapping the source texture produces different faces. [creator statement](https://store.steampowered.com/news/posts/?appids=433340&enddate=1576525914&feed=steam_community_announcements)

MIST experiments:

- Our inference: keep MIST's familiar eyes and body, and author a small set of clearly different mouth silhouettes before adding more continuous movement.
- Our inference: separate gaze, eye aperture, and mouth state so a listening response can move the eyes first and change the mouth only after recognition. The delayed timing is our proposal, not a measured property of this GIF.
- Our inference: use wobble as a small secondary effect; it should not replace authored reactions or make every state equally active.

Sources:

- [Slime Faces: Behind The Scenes, 25 June 2019](https://store.steampowered.com/news/posts/?appids=433340&enddate=1576525914&feed=steam_community_announcements)

GIF URL extracted from the rendered developer post and loaded successfully. A displayed frame was inspected; continuous motion was not perceived or measured. McConville is not relabelled as a credited character animator.

## Dicey Dungeons / Lady Luck

An official dialogue GIF gives a clear comparison between composed, knowing attention and open excitement in the same scene.

- [Marlowe Dobbe](https://diceydungeons.com/credits.html): Art and Animation

- In the inspected GIF frame, Lady Luck has a half-lidded sideways eye, a high curved brow and a small angular smile. The warrior has open eyes, raised brows and an open grin. Their attitudes remain distinct at a glance. [visual observation](https://terrycavanaghgames.com/dice/presskit/xbox/)

MIST experiments:

- Our inference: build a mildly amused listening pose around eyelid height and sideways gaze, then keep that eye pose while the mouth changes for speech.
- Our inference: distinguish alert delight from ordinary warmth with eye opening and brow height before increasing mouth size. Avoid giving every positive state the warrior's excited grin.

## OMORI / pencil portrait

The supplied portrait GIF keeps a nearly neutral face while the contours change slightly, showing how a held expression can remain visibly drawn without constant emotional movement.

- [OMOCAT](https://www.omocat.com/pagecv): OMORI Game Director, Illustrator, Writer, Programmer, Animator / Video Editor, among other project roles listed in the artist CV; individual GIF authorship is not separately itemized.

- The artist's portfolio GIF has a frontal head, dark oval eyes, gently angled brows and a very small mouth. Across its three decoded frames, the same neutral expression remains while hair, shoulders and contours shift slightly. [visual observation](https://www.omocat.com/omori)
- The mouth remains tiny relative to the eyes and dark hair mass. The drawing does not need a wide mouth or a smile to read as a face; this is useful when a character is listening or withholding a response. [visual observation](https://www.omocat.com/omori)
- OMOCAT's portfolio CV explicitly includes animation and video editing among their OMORI roles. It also credits illustration separately, so this is stronger evidence than inferring animation work from a general art credit. [creator statement](https://www.omocat.com/pagecv)

MIST experiments:

- Our inference: design a readable listening pose with a very small mouth and stable gaze, then allow only a faint surface or contour variation if it suits MIST's existing material.
- Our inference: reserve a blink, gaze change or mouth opening for a conversational event. A held emotional pose and visual texture can be controlled independently.
- Our inference: do not copy pencil jitter directly onto a robot display without testing. The transferable choice is restrained acting; the handmade texture is optional.

Sources:

- [OMORI, 2014-2020, OMOCAT portfolio](https://www.omocat.com/omori)
- [OMOCAT CV](https://www.omocat.com/pagecv)

Main GIF's three stored frames were decoded and inspected individually; this verifies drawing differences but not continuous motion perception. motionVerified remains false. The frame count describes this supplied portfolio GIF only, not a game production specification. No clip timestamps or dialogue cue timings were verified.

## In Stars and Time / Odile

A half-smile and tired, pinched eyes make reassurance sound dry and complicated before the sentence is finished.

- [Adrienne Bazir (insertdisc5)](https://www.insertdisc5.com/about): Game developer and cartoonist with 2D animation experience; developer of In Stars and Time. Individual portrait animation credit not established here.

- In the official Odile screenshot, the mouth lifts in a small uneven smile while the eyes remain heavily lidded. The inner brow area pinches upward. The combination reads as wry concern rather than uncomplicated happiness; that emotional reading is our visual interpretation. [visual observation](https://armor-games-studios.itch.io/in-stars-and-time)
- The portrait reaches above the dialogue box and includes an angled upper body. This lets the face retain its fine eye and mouth shapes while the torso supplies a relaxed attitude. [visual observation](https://armor-games-studios.itch.io/in-stars-and-time)
- The official battle screenshot gives four simultaneous face treatments: a narrow-eyed smirk, a worried mouth with sweat, an active open-mouth attack pose, and a downturned tired face. Their differences remain readable without hue. [visual observation](https://armor-games-studios.itch.io/in-stars-and-time)

MIST experiments:

- Our inference: author a reassuring-but-unsure pose with a small smile, lowered lids and a slight inward lift at the brow; avoid moving every feature toward a single happy/sad label.
- Our inference: use a three-pose cue sequence for a difficult answer: attentive hold, wry acknowledgement at the qualifying clause, then a quieter recovery. Exact durations would need testing; none are measured from this screenshot.
- Our inference: judge the expression in grayscale and at final display size before adding color or effects. Preserve MIST's silhouette while changing the relationship between lid angle and mouth asymmetry.

## UNDERTALE / Flowey

An official sequence pairs simple smiling portraits with denial, embarrassment and a remembered act of care, giving useful examples of a face saying more than its words.

- [Toby Fox](https://toby.fangamer.com/interviews/temmie/): UNDERTALE creator and interviewer; exact authorship of the selected Alarm Clock portrait pixels is not established by the page.
- [Temmie Chang](https://undertale.com/deltarune-update-092021/): UNDERTALE art collaborator; separately credited as Main Artist and Main Animator on DELTARUNE Chapter 2. This is not an attribution of the Flowey portraits to her.

- The bicycle sequence changes the eye and mouth treatment while preserving Flowey's flower outline, stem and overall placement. A narrowed-eyed expression accompanies his hypothetical gift; later the face returns to a plain open-eyed smile when the desired bicycle is specified. [visual observation](https://undertale.com/alarmclock/flowey/)
- In the inspected panels, a large-eyed smile accompanies the claim that a gift would waste everyone's time. This mismatch between friendly mouth and defensive language is a useful mixed-affect reading, not a claim about a documented production method. [visual observation](https://undertale.com/alarmclock/flowey/)
- The published panel order includes standalone pauses before and after a painful memory. These are readable dialogue beats on a static page; the page does not establish how many milliseconds the game would hold them. [visual observation](https://undertale.com/alarmclock/flowey/)
- In Toby Fox's interview, Temmie recalls being impressed by Flowey's acting and laugh in an early demo. This is evidence of the creators' attention to acting, not proof that Temmie animated the scene. [creator statement](https://toby.fangamer.com/interviews/temmie/)

MIST experiments:

- Our inference: keep the body or chassis nearly fixed during a spoken thought, then change the face when the intention changes. This gives a small eye or mouth change more weight.
- Our inference: for an awkward admission, hold a socially pleasant mouth while the eyes narrow or avert, insert a still pause, then let the mouth soften. This should be authored as an acting cue, not a generic idle loop.
- Our inference: separate speech-mouth motion from the expressive mouth pose so the expression can remain readable during silence. Use the phrase boundary and the emotional turn as cue inputs.
