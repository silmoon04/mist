Original experiment based on the developer's reported work on gaze, blinking, facial expressions and lip sync for Farewell North. No shot-level face performance was inspected, so do not imitate or invent the game's human face style. Build five independent MIST dialogue poses that expose three authored controls clearly: gaze direction, lid aperture and restrained mouth silhouette. Use clean cyan outlined oval or rounded-capsule eye apertures with one simple cyan pupil each inside dark space; eye outline and pupil belong together, no extra marks. Comfortable graphic scale lets the reactions read when viewed smaller.
Pose 1: upright eye apertures, pupils directed gently toward the viewer, relaxed symmetrical eyelids, a short closed level mouth with softly rounded ends.
Pose 2: pupils look up-right, the right lid slightly tighter, a tiny closed slanted mouth suggesting thought.
Pose 3: low horizontal oval apertures with lowered upper lids, pupils directed aside, mouth short and flat with one corner pushed downward, visibly skeptical.

Pose 5: eye apertures tilt inward with low pupils and softened lids, small gently downturned mouth, concerned listening rather than speaking.
