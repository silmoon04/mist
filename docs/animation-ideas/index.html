How we replace the pack
  1. Choose the key poses and lock the eye shapes, spacing, colour and mouth style.
  2. Draw eyes and mouths separately, with in-between frames and deliberate holds at 15 fps.
  3. Check transitions, loop seams, interruptions and mouth timing against audible speech.
  4. Enable one verified family at a time, keeping the old assets as a fallback.

Start with idle, listening, thinking and transitions. Speech follows, then task loops and reactions. Complete loops will use 12 or more drawn cels; a blink only needs enough closing poses to feel natural. Timings below are proposals, not measured playback.
